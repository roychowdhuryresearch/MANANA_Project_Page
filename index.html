01 / THE CHALLENGE

The right knowledge.
The wrong defaults.

A treatment recommendation can be medically plausible and still miss the realities of local care. Medication availability, prescribing practices, and follow-up patterns differ across clinical settings.

Working with clinicians in Uganda, we study whether language models can learn those differences from a small set of local patients. The task: predict anti-seizure medication regimens from unstructured notes across successive clinic visits.
